# Forest Cabin

A charming single-bedroom retreat nestled in nature. Built from reclaimed wood with modern amenities and a stunning forest outlook from every window.

## Key Features

- **Reclaimed timber** exterior with authentic character and warmth
- **High ceilings** with skylights flooding the interior with natural light
- **Loft sleeping area** maximising the compact footprint
- **Covered porch** perfect for year-round outdoor dining
- **Modern bathroom** with rainfall shower
- **Energy-efficient** design with excellent insulation values

## Perfect for Nature Lovers

The Forest Cabin is our most intimate model — a haven for those who want to disconnect and immerse themselves in nature. Despite its modest size, clever design choices make every square metre count.

The main living area features high ceilings with two strategically placed skylights that frame views of the tree canopy above. The kitchen is fully equipped with everything you need, and the covered porch extends your living space outdoors in all but the harshest weather.

The sleeping loft is accessed by a beautifully crafted timber staircase and can comfortably accommodate a king-sized bed with storage underneath.
