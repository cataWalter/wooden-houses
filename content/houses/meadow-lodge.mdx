# Meadow Lodge

A spacious family home with three bedrooms, two bathrooms, and a generous wraparound deck. Ideal for permanent living or a luxury holiday property.

## Key Features

- **120 m²** of beautifully designed living space
- **Three bedrooms** including master suite with en-suite bathroom
- **Open-plan kitchen** and dining area with island
- **Large wraparound deck** on three sides of the house
- **Double-aspect living room** with wood-burning fireplace
- **Utility room** with laundry facilities
- **Underfloor heating** throughout

## Room to Grow

The Meadow Lodge is our most popular family model. With three generous bedrooms and two bathrooms, it offers the space and privacy that families need.

The heart of the home is the open-plan kitchen and dining area, which flows seamlessly onto the wraparound deck through large sliding glass doors. This indoor-outdoor connection makes the Lodge feel even larger than its 120 m².

The master bedroom suite includes a walk-in wardrobe and en-suite bathroom with a freestanding bathtub. The two additional bedrooms share a family bathroom with shower.
