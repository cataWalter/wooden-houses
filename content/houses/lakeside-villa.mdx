# Lakeside Villa

Our most prestigious model — a four-bedroom lakeside villa with panoramic water views, a private jetty, and a stunning open-plan living space designed for entertaining.

## Key Features

- **160 m²** of premium living space across two floors
- **Four bedrooms** — master suite with lake-view balcony, three further doubles
- **Two bathrooms** — master en-suite with freestanding bath, family bathroom with double shower
- **Grand open-plan** kitchen, dining, and living area with 4-metre ceilings
- **Floor-to-ceiling glazing** on the lake-facing elevation
- **Private jetty** and boat storage (plot-dependent)
- **Underfloor heating** throughout ground floor
- **Integrated home automation** — lighting, heating, and security

## Designed for Lakeside Living

The Lakeside Villa is our flagship model, designed specifically for waterfront plots. The entire south-facing elevation is glazed, creating a seamless connection between the interior and the water beyond.

The ground floor is entirely open-plan — kitchen, dining, and living flow together in one magnificent space. A central wood-burning fireplace anchors the room and provides warmth and atmosphere in the cooler months.

Upstairs, the master suite occupies the full width of the lake-facing elevation, with a private balcony that runs the length of the house. Three further bedrooms are arranged around a central landing, each with built-in storage and views of the surrounding landscape.

## Customisation Options

The Lakeside Villa can be configured with:
- **Sauna** (integrated into the lower ground floor)
- **Covered boat garage** (attached to the main structure)
- **Rooftop terrace** (replacing the standard pitched roof section)
- **Solar panel array** (integrated into the roof structure)
