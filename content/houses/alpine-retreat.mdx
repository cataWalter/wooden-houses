# Alpine Retreat

A cozy 85 m² wooden house perfect for mountain living. Crafted from sustainably sourced Nordic pine with premium insulation for year-round comfort.

## Key Features

- **Premium Nordic Pine** construction with hand-finished interiors
- **Triple-glazed windows** for exceptional thermal and acoustic insulation
- **Open-plan living area** with cathedral ceiling and exposed beams
- **Wrap-around terrace** with panoramic mountain views
- **Wood-burning stove** for cozy winter evenings
- **Fully equipped kitchen** with oak cabinetry

## Why Choose the Alpine Retreat?

The Alpine Retreat is designed for those who want to escape the city without sacrificing comfort. Its compact footprint makes it perfect for mountain plots while delivering a surprisingly spacious interior feel.

The open-plan layout connects the kitchen, dining, and living areas — all bathed in natural light from the floor-to-ceiling south-facing windows. In winter, the high-efficiency wood stove keeps the whole space warm and inviting.

Both bedrooms feature built-in storage and can accommodate queen-sized beds. The master bedroom has direct access to the terrace, perfect for morning coffee with mountain views.
