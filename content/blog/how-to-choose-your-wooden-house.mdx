# How to Choose the Right Wooden House for Your Land

Buying a wooden house is an exciting journey, but the right choice depends heavily on your specific plot and circumstances. This guide walks you through the key considerations.

## Start with Your Plot

**Orientation matters.** South-facing plots maximise passive solar gain, reducing heating costs significantly. Our Alpine Retreat, with its large south-facing glazing, is ideally suited to plots with unobstructed southern exposure.

**Slope and drainage.** Sloped plots can be advantageous — they allow for under-house storage or garaging, and improve natural drainage. However, they require more careful foundation work.

**Access.** Consider how your house will be delivered and assembled. Our prefabricated sections are large but manageable — we need vehicle access to within approximately 20 metres of the final position.

## Know Your Planning Rules

Planning regulations vary significantly by region and country. Key questions to ask your local authority:

- What is the maximum permitted footprint?
- Are there height restrictions?
- Do any aesthetic requirements apply (roof pitch, cladding colour, window proportions)?
- Is the land in a protected landscape or conservation area?

We can provide documentation and specifications to support your planning application.

## Match the Model to Your Needs

**For solo occupiers or couples:** The Forest Cabin offers everything you need in a beautifully compact package. Its low purchase price also makes it an attractive holiday rental investment.

**For families:** The Meadow Lodge with its three bedrooms and two bathrooms is designed for family life. The large wraparound deck effectively extends the living area in warmer months.

**For mountain or rural plots:** The Alpine Retreat excels in challenging climates. Its steep roof pitch handles heavy snow loads, and the high-performance thermal envelope keeps heating costs minimal even at altitude.

## Think About the Future

Wooden houses are adaptable. All our models can be extended with additional modules as your needs change. Consider whether you might want to add a home office, extra bedroom, or covered outdoor space in future — and choose a model and plot that can accommodate that growth.
